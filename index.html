01 / Introduction

About me

I am a second-year M.S. student in Computer Science and Technology at Wuhan University, where I am advised by Prof. Xingping Dong and Prof. Heng Fan.

My research interests include 3D vision, Real2Sim, image editing, few-shot learning, and semi-supervised learning. My recent work explores reconstructing interactive, simulation-ready 3D scenes from a single image, with a focus on articulated objects and scene-level consistency.
